Why AxiomVision Outperforms Vision LLMs

Mathematical Sub-Pixel Precision
Pure-Rust 3x3 Laplacian Unsharp Mask sharpens tiny 9-10px terminal fonts and badges before AVX2 SIMD extraction, eliminating fuzzy token hallucinations.
Live AT-SPI2 Memory Fusion
Instead of guessing what an icon or button says, AxiomVision queries the OS accessibility tree directly over D-Bus to fuse 100% exact UTF-8 ground-truth strings.
100% In-Memory (Zero-Disk)
Screenshots stay in RAM buffers and stream over pipes. Zero temporary PNG files are written to disk, guaranteeing zero flash, zero shutter sounds, and maximum privacy.
123+ Languages Just-In-Time
Repository ships at only 40MB with English baseline. Any of the 123+ fast quantized models are installed on-demand in under 1 second without bloating disk.
